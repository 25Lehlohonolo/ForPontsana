FOR TIEGO

♥

My Tiego,

I don't think there is one perfect way to explain what you mean to me.

Somehow, somewhere along the way, you became my person.

My B.

My Pontsana.

Yes... my Pontsana.

I know you don't always like that name, but I hope you know how much love there is behind it.

When I think about us, I don't only think about the big moments.

I think about the little things. The smiles. The laughs. The random conversations. The moments that weren't planned but somehow became memories.

Those are the moments I would never want to lose.

♥

You have become such a beautiful part of my life, Tiego.

And if I could go back and choose again...

I would still choose you.

Not because everything will always be perfect.

But because you're the person I want beside me while we figure everything out.

I want to keep making memories with you.

I want more laughs. More random moments. More pictures. More stories that only make sense to us.

I want to keep writing our story.

And I want you in every chapter.

So if there is one thing I hope you remember from this little world I made for you...

There is someone here who chooses you.

Again. And again. And again.

And if I had to choose you a thousand times...

I would choose you a thousand times.

Forever your B,

Lehlohonolo ❤️
Written with love ♥ For Tiego
